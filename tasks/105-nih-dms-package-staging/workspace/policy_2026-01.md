# Synthetic DMS package policy — effective 2026-01-25

This offline exercise adapts NIH repository-selection and participant-privacy
workflows. It stages local bytes for institutional review; it does not upload,
deposit, approve, email, or contact a repository.

## Authority and timing

- Apply this snapshot because `project.json` names `2026-01-25`; the draft's
  older snapshot, one-repository preference, fallback, and six-month delay are
  not controlling.
- Participant-level scientific data must be planned for availability no later
  than the associated publication date. A timing or repository-split change is
  material and keeps `institutional_review` pending.
- Every source asset appears exactly once in the revised plan and final local
  manifest. A previous interrupted copy is not approval evidence.

## Consent and route rules

- C1 participant survey bytes may be staged only as `controlled_file` to an
  external repository supporting controlled files, a DUA workflow, and an
  access committee. Its dictionary is non-participant documentation and may be
  a `public_file`.
- C2 voice and transcript files are `excluded_external_file` even if a
  transcript was de-identified or an internal archive accepts it. They remain
  source evidence only and may not appear anywhere under the final external
  `staging/` tree. C2 `audio_metadata` may be a public
  `metadata_only_record` at a capable external repository.
- Code and its environment inventory travel together to one external
  code-capable repository as `code_file`. Aggregate model outputs are a
  `public_file` at a public-file repository.
- A repository is usable only if every required capability is true. An
  internal archive cannot satisfy external sharing, and accepting a controlled
  file without DUA and access-committee support is insufficient.

## Plan and review state

`deposit_plan.json` has `schema_version`, `plan_id`, `policy_effective`,
`plan_revision`, `publication_release_date`, `assets`, `approval_gates`,
`repository_split_required`, and `external_deposit_executed`. Each asset row
has `asset_id`, `disposition`, `repository`, `access`, and `evidence_refs`.
Use `repository: internal_only` for excluded bytes. Evidence references are
visible `basename#identifier` values separated in a JSON array.

The only disposition values are `controlled_file`, `public_file`,
`metadata_only_record`, `code_file`, and `excluded_external_file`. The three
approval gates `plan_revision`, `privacy_review`, and `institutional_review`
must all remain `pending`; `external_deposit_executed` is false. A correct
local build replaces, rather than trusts, the interrupted staging tree.
