# Degree audit policy

Use the 2026-CS catalog as of 2026-08-16. Passed institutional courses satisfy
exact requirements. A transfer equivalency must be valid on the audit date;
expired mappings cannot be revived. Approved substitutions satisfy only their
named requirement. One course cannot satisfy two requirements. Prerequisite
eligibility recognizes valid equivalencies and requires every parent to be
completed; eligibility does not satisfy the future course. This offline audit
does not register, enroll, confer, or update a live student record.
