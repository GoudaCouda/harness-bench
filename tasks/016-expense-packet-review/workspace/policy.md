# Reimbursement policy — July 2026 close

- The employee and service date must fall within an approved travel authorization. A missing authorization is rejected, not assumed pending.
- Airfare is reimbursed at actual cost. Airline change fees above USD 200 need an approved Travel Director exception for the same trip and expense.
- Lodging room charge is capped at USD 300 per night. Itemized occupancy taxes remain allowable in addition to the room cap; minibar and personal incidentals are not allowable.
- Meals use the lower of claimed amount and per-diem cap: breakfast USD 25, lunch USD 40, dinner USD 75. A meal provided by a conference or host is not reimbursable. Per-diem meals do not require a receipt.
- Ground transport is actual cost. A receipt is required when a single ride is over USD 25.
- Other foreign-currency expenses use the service-date rate in `fx_rates.csv`.
- A corporate-card transaction may support only one reimbursement line. A copy of the same receipt or reuse of the same card transaction is a duplicate and receives USD 0.
- Personal spend receives USD 0. A required receipt or approval that is absent makes an otherwise eligible line `pending` with USD 0 until cured.
