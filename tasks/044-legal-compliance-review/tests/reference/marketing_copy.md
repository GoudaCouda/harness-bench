# BrightCart Spring Campaign

BrightCart helps teams understand checkout friction, review campaign
performance, and prioritize retention experiments. Results vary by store,
campaign setup, and available data.

Compared with a team's prior spreadsheet workflow, BrightCart may help reviewers
find checkout issues faster when campaign tags are kept current. This is a
qualitative workflow description, not a universal performance promise.

Qualifying stores can start with no platform fee during the first year. Annual
renewal surcharges may apply after year one under each executed order form.

For migration compatibility, older ecommerce and ERP exports retain the literal
field names `guaranteed_delivery_flag` and `risk_free_returns_schema`. These
are technical identifiers, not performance or shopper promises.
