# Subtitle QC policy

Cues are consecutively numbered from 1 and reproduce the approved transcript
exactly. Each cue lasts 1000–3000 ms, adjacent cues have at least a 100 ms gap,
and a cue must end before the next listed shot cut rather than cross it. The
first cue begins at 1000 ms; use the cut-safe windows exposed by the evidence.
The repair tool is offline and cannot publish media.
