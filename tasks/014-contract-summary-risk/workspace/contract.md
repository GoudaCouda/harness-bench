# Master Services Agreement — counterparty draft 4

**Provider:** Acme Analytics Inc.
**Customer:** Baxter Retail LLC

Clause C-01 — Term. The initial term is 24 months beginning 2026-07-01. The
agreement automatically renews for successive 12-month terms unless either
party gives 30 calendar days' notice before the renewal date.

Clause C-02 — Payment. Invoices are payable net 60 from invoice date. Late fees
are waived.

Clause C-03 — Data and incidents. Acme may store customer data in any region
used by its subprocessors. Acme will notify Baxter of a confirmed security
incident within 10 business days.

Clause C-04 — Liability. Acme's aggregate liability is unlimited for all claims
arising under this agreement.

Clause C-05 — Termination. Baxter may terminate for convenience with 15
calendar days' notice. Acme may suspend service for non-payment after 20 days'
notice.

Clause C-06 — Subprocessors. Acme will give 5 calendar days' advance notice of
a new subprocessor.

Clause C-07 — Assignment. Neither party may assign the agreement without the
other party's consent, except in a merger or sale of substantially all assets.

Clause C-08 — Publicity. The order form may grant either party a standing right
to use the other party's name or logo in customer lists.
