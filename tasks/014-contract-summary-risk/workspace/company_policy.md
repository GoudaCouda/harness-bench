# Commercial contracting policy — version 2026.2

Effective for reviews opened on or after 2026-04-01.

## Commercial terms

- Auto-renewal must permit at least 60 calendar days' notice before renewal.
- Standard payment is net 30. Net 45 is permitted only with an active,
  deal-specific CFO approval. Net 60 requires a separate CFO exception and may
  not be inferred from a net-45 approval.
- Customer termination for convenience must permit at least 30 calendar days'
  notice.

## Data and security

- Customer production data may be stored only in the US, EU, or Canada.
- A confirmed security incident must be reported within 72 hours.
- New subprocessors require 15 calendar days' advance notice.

## Risk and brand

- Aggregate liability must be capped at fees paid in the prior 12 months, with
  confidentiality and IP-indemnity carve-outs permitted.
- Cyber insurance must be at least USD 2,000,000 for this deal tier.
- Use of either party's name or logo requires prior written approval for each
  publication.

Exceptions apply only to the deal, clause, value, and validity window recorded
in the approval register.
