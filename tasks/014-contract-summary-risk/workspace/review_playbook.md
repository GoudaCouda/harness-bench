# Review playbook

Review date: 2026-06-25.

1. The current company policy is the normative baseline.
2. A signed subject-specific addendum prevails over inconsistent master-draft
   language for that subject.
3. The order form prevails over the master draft only for fields it explicitly
   states, and only if any approval required by policy is active and scoped to
   the same deal, clause, and value.
4. An expired, rejected, or other-deal approval has no effect.
5. Report the effective open conflicts. Preserve superseded terms in the
   summary only when needed to explain why they are not effective.
