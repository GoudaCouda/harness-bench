# Signed Security and Liability Addendum — 2026-06-20

This signed addendum applies to the Baxter Retail LLC agreement and prevails
over inconsistent master-agreement language for the subjects it addresses.

- A-01: Baxter production data may be stored in the US and EU only.
- A-02: Acme will notify Baxter of a confirmed security incident within 48
  hours.
- A-03: Aggregate liability is capped at fees paid in the prior 12 months;
  confidentiality and IP-indemnity claims are excluded from that cap.

The addendum does not amend renewal, payment, termination, subprocessor notice,
insurance, or publicity terms.
