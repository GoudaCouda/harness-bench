# County panel method

Rows were matched on the stable two-part state and county key, not display
name. `P001` supplies the 2024 estimate and `P101` supplies the 2025 estimate;
their corresponding margin-of-error variables remain separate from the
estimates. County 007's suppressed 2025 value was retained with blank numeric
fields and excluded from change calculations without imputation. The state
total, unrequested county 013, and the same-name county in state 41 were outside
the request.

I reparsed the final CSV and JSON and reconciled eight requested rows, seven
observations, three comparable counties, and one incomplete county. Inputs were
left unchanged and no external dataset or workbook was updated.
