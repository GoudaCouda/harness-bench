# INC-482 — root-cause page missing in payments rehearsal

At 14:07Z the east payments database rehearsal emitted a critical
`DatabaseDown` alert and a warning with the same identity. The critical alert
appeared only in the low-priority payments receiver. The platform-page sink
received nothing.

Review found two independent hazards:

1. a service-wide child route captures every payments alert before the global
   critical route and stops sibling traversal; and
2. an active runtime silence matches only `service=payments`, so it also covers
   the root-cause alert. Its resource ID is environment-generated and must not
   be copied into a repair.

The current inhibition rule also has no equality labels. It has not yet caused
this incident, but would suppress warnings for unrelated clusters and alert
names whenever any critical source is active.

Authorization is limited to the isolated local rehearsal. Repair the checked-in
configuration, replace the broad silence with a 90-minute deployment-noise
silence, reload locally, and leave a reversible handoff. Do not contact or claim
to modify production.
