# S06 Community Scheduling Pilot Note

## Observed results

A six-month scheduling pilot at three community sites recorded an on-time visit rate of 81% before the pilot and 92% during the pilot. The pilot had no randomized control group, and staffing also changed during the observation window.

The implementation cost recorded by the pilot team was CNY 1.8 million. The pilot team estimated annual operating savings of CNY 1.2 million. Dividing implementation cost by estimated annual savings gives a simple payback period of 1.5 years.

## Interpretation limit

The observation supports a limited follow-up pilot. It does not establish that the scheduling system caused the improvement, and it does not guarantee that the savings will recur citywide.
