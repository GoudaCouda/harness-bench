# RF-77 / RF-77A signed determination

Signed 2025-08-18 by the regional transport authority. RF-77 authorizes
Project Kestrel to begin **limited commercial service on 2025-09-01** with
Northstar Mobility as the certified primary operator for phase one.

RF-77A limits that launch-day authorization to the **East Market corridor**.
The two airport connector trips described in the commercial contract remain
outside the authorization until stop-signage certification is recorded. This
determination says nothing about capital allocation, customer population, or
the legal approval state of customer communications.
