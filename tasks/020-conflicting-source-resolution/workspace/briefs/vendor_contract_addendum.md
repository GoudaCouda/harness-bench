# Contract addendum CA-14

Executed 2025-08-11. Northstar Mobility is the primary operator. Altair
Transit is a fallback maintenance subcontractor for non-critical stop signage.
The contracted phase-one scope includes East Market plus two airport connector
trips, subject to public-authority certification.

COI-14 is required before airport connector work can start and is still under
review. The addendum does not grant regulatory authorization or approve public
communications.
